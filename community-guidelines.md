# Community Guidelines

Last updated: 16.11.2026

We want this to be a respectful and constructive space for music discussion. These guidelines apply to reviews, comments, profiles, lists and direct messages, and are part of our [Terms of Use](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/terms-of-use).

---

## Be Respectful

- Criticize music, not people
- No harassment, hate speech, threats or personal attacks
- Don't keep messaging people who don't want to hear from you

---

## Be Honest

- Share genuine opinions
- No spam, advertising or fake reviews
- Don't manipulate ratings, for example with multiple accounts
- Don't pretend to be someone else

---

## Copyright

- Do not upload copyrighted material you do not own
- No full song lyrics unless permitted

---

## Keep It Safe

- No illegal content
- No sexually explicit or violent material
- Zero tolerance for anything that sexualizes or endangers minors (see our [Child Safety Standards](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/child-safety-standards))

---

## Moderation

- Report reviews, comments and users in the App, or email listened.app.support@gmail.com
- Reports are reviewed by a person
- Violations may result in content removal, restricted features or account suspension
- You can block users you don't want to interact with

---

Thank you for helping keep the community welcoming and respectful.
