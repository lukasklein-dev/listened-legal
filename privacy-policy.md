# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22.10.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("Listened.").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Username
- Password (stored securely using encryption)

Email verification is required to activate your account.

---

### 1.2 User Generated Content

We collect and store content you voluntarily provide, including:

- Music ratings
- Reviews
- Profile information such as username and optional profile picture.

This content may be publicly visible to other users.

---

### 1.3 Usage & Technical Data

We may collect limited technical data such as:

- Device type
- App version
- Crash logs

This data is used solely to improve app stability and performance.

We also read your device's region (a country code such as "DE") to show music charts for your country. It is sent only to our own servers to request those charts and is not stored with your account.

---

### 1.4 Personalization of the "For You" Feed

To tailor the "For You" feed to you, the App records how you use it:

- Which items (ratings, reviews and album suggestions) were shown to you
- How long each item stayed on your screen
- Whether you liked, opened, saved, shared or rated it, listened to its preview, or marked it as "Not interested"
- The artist, genre and author of the item

Together with your favorite genres and artists, your ratings and the users and artists you follow, this is used to recommend content that matches your taste and to not show you the same items again.

This data:

- Is only visible to you and is used only for these recommendations
- Is never shown to other users, sold, or used for advertising
- Is deleted automatically after 90 days, and together with your account when it is deleted

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Display ratings and reviews
- Personalize your "For You" feed and other recommendations
- Moderate content
- Improve app performance and security
- Comply with legal obligations

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

All data is securely stored using **Supabase** infrastructure.

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data.

Your data may be shared only with:

- Infrastructure providers (e.g. Supabase)
- Legal authorities if required by law

---

## 5. User Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct your data
- Delete your account and associated data

Account deletion can be initiated **directly within the app**. Account deletion is subject to a 14-day grace period during which the user may cancel the deletion by logging in again.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Your data is retained only as long as your account is active or as required by law.

Usage data of the "For You" feed (see 1.4) is kept for at most 90 days.

Once deleted, your data will be permanently removed within a reasonable timeframe.

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for users under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect data from children.

---

## 8. Third-Party Services

The App may display music metadata, such as album and artist information, charts and short song previews, provided by third-party services such as Apple Music. Most of this is requested by our servers. Song previews, and music data while our servers are unavailable, are loaded by your device directly from the provider, which, like any website, then sees your IP address. We do not share your account data, ratings or usage data with these services.

We do not provide full music streaming.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes will be effective upon publication.
