## 3. Data we process

For each purpose we name the legal basis under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): needed to provide Listened to you under our [Terms of Use](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/terms-of-use).
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): needed for an interest of ours that outweighs yours, such as security, stability and a safe community. You can object to it (see section 8).
- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): only where you agreed, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): where the law requires it.

### 3.1 Account

When you sign up we process:

- Your email address
- Your password, stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text
- Your username
- How you sign in (email, Google or Apple)

If you sign in with **Google** or **Apple**, they share your email address with us and, depending on the provider and your choices, your name and profile picture address. With Apple's "Hide My Email" we only receive a relay address. Your name from Google or Apple is not shown in the App.

We send emails needed for your account, such as the confirmation link and the password reset link. We don't send newsletters or marketing emails.

Legal basis: contract. Stored until your account is deleted.

### 3.2 Profile and content

We store what you add to Listened:

- Profile: username, profile picture, bio, country, favorite genres, instrument, shoutout artist, top albums and top artists
- Ratings and reviews (including sub-ratings, vibes and favorite track), comments, likes, lists and saved lists
- Follows and followers

This content is **public**: every Listened user can see it. When you or someone else shares a review, list or profile via a link, it is also visible on our share pages on the web without an account, including in link previews of messaging apps.

A profile picture is only uploaded once you pick one from your photo library. The App only receives the photo you select.

Legal basis: contract. Stored until you delete the content or your account.

### 3.3 Direct messages

We store the messages you send and receive, shared items, likes on messages and message requests. Messages are only visible to the people in the conversation.

We do not read your messages. If someone reports you from a conversation, the report includes your most recent messages in that conversation so that we can check it (see 3.7).

Legal basis: contract. Stored until your account or the other person's account is deleted.

### 3.4 Personalization of the "For You" feed

To tailor the "For You" feed to you, the App records how you use it:

- Which items (ratings, reviews and album suggestions) were shown to you
- How long each item stayed on your screen
- Whether you liked, opened, saved, shared or rated it, listened to its preview, or marked it as "Not interested"
- The artist, genre and author of the item

Together with your favorite genres and artists, your ratings and the users and artists you follow, this is used to recommend content that matches your taste and to not show you the same items again.

This data is only visible to you, used only for these recommendations, never shown to other users, sold or used for advertising. It is deleted automatically after **90 days**, and together with your account. The recommendations have no legal or similarly significant effect on you (no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR).

Legal basis: contract (the personalized feed is a feature of Listened).

### 3.5 Usage and technical data

- **Activity and app version:** when you open the App we store the time of your last activity, your platform (iOS or Android), the platform you signed up on and the installed app version. We use this for support, to plan releases and to see which versions are still in use. Legal basis: legitimate interest. Stored until your account is deleted.
- **Invitations:** if you sign up after opening a link shared by another user, we store which user shared it. This is only visible to us and helps us understand how Listened spreads. Legal basis: legitimate interest. Removed when either account is deleted.
- **Device region:** we read your device's region (a country code such as "DE") to show music charts for your country. It is sent only to our servers to request those charts and is not stored with your account. Legal basis: contract.
- **Server logs:** when the App or a share page connects to our servers, our hosting providers process your IP address, the time and the requested address to deliver the content and to protect against abuse. Their logs are kept only for a short period. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
- **App updates:** the App checks with Expo's servers for updates. Expo receives your IP address, your platform and the app version. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

### 3.6 Push notifications

If you allow notifications in the system dialog, we store a push token for your device. Notifications are delivered via Expo and the push services of Apple (APNs) or Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). They receive the token and the notification text.

You can turn notifications off in the App's settings or in your device settings at any time. The token is removed when you sign out and when your account is deleted.

Legal basis: consent.

### 3.7 Reports, blocks and moderation

When you report content or block a user, we store who reported or blocked whom, the reason and the details you entered. We are notified by email (via Resend) with the report, the reported content and, for reports from a conversation, the reported user's most recent messages in that conversation.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in a safe community and legal obligation (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Digital Services Act). Stored until the reporting account is deleted. Notification emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the case.

### 3.8 Feedback and support

- **Feedback form:** we store your category, title and message together with your account, and are notified by email (via Resend).
- **Support emails:** "Contact Support" opens your email app with a prepared email to our support address. It includes the app version, device model, operating system, your username and user ID so that we can help you faster. You can remove these lines before sending.

Legal basis: contract and legitimate interest. Kept as long as needed to handle your request.

### 3.9 Storage on your device

The App stores on your device what it needs to work: your login session, your language and settings (such as haptic feedback and preview looping), your recent searches, and a pending invitation from a shared link until you sign up. This data stays on your device; there are no cookies, no advertising IDs and no tracking.

Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the service you requested), and contract.

### 3.10 Music data, previews and links

Album and artist information, artwork, charts and short song previews come from third parties, mainly Apple Music. Artist facts come from Wikipedia and Wikidata, and links to streaming services are looked up at Spotify and Deezer.

Most of this is requested by our servers without any personal data. Song previews and artwork, and music data while our servers are unavailable, are loaded by your device directly from Apple, which like any website then sees your IP address. When you open a link to Apple Music, Spotify or another service, their own privacy policies apply.

We do not provide full music streaming.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above:

- Account, profile, content and messages: until you delete them or your account
- "For You" usage data: at most 90 days
- Database backups: 30 days

Account deletion is subject to a 14-day grace period during which you can cancel it by signing in again. Details: [Account Deletion](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/account-deletion).

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data and receive a copy (Art. 15 GDPR)
- **Correct** inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- **Delete** your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
- **Restrict** processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- **Receive** your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
- **Withdraw** your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)
- **Lodge a complaint** with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or where we are based (Art. 77 GDPR)

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR):** where we process your data based on legitimate interest, you can object at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation.

You can edit your profile, delete individual content and delete your account directly in the App. For everything else, email us at listened.app.support@gmail.com. We answer within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first.
