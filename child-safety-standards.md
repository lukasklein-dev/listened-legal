# Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 07.10.2026

**Listened.** has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

---

## Our standards

- Any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits or endangers minors is strictly prohibited. This applies to reviews, comments, profile content (including profile pictures), lists and direct messages.
- Grooming, solicitation of minors and sharing or requesting CSAM are prohibited.
- Listened is not intended for children under 13 (see our [Privacy Policy](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/privacy-policy)).

These standards are part of our [Terms of Use](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/terms-of-use) and [Community Guidelines](https://lukasklein-dev.github.io/listened-legal/community-guidelines).

---

## How to report

- **In the app:** use the report option on reviews and comments, or send us a message via **Settings → Contact Support** or **Settings → Send Feedback**.
- **By email:** contact **listened.app.support@gmail.com** with the subject "Child safety". Include the username and a description of the content. Please don't attach the illegal material itself.

---

## How we respond

- We review reports about child safety with the highest priority.
- We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
- We report CSAM and other child sexual exploitation to the relevant authorities. This includes the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and national law enforcement, as required by applicable law.
- We cooperate with law enforcement requests in accordance with applicable law.

---

## Compliance

We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations.
