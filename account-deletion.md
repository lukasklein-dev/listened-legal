# Delete Your Listened Account

Last updated: 07.10.2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the **Listened.** app and what happens to your data.

---

## Option 1: In the app (recommended)

1. Open Listened and sign in.
2. Go to your **Profile** tab and open **Settings** (gear icon).
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**, then confirm.

You are signed out immediately. Your account is permanently deleted after a **14-day grace period**. If you sign in again during these 14 days, the deletion is cancelled.

---

## Option 2: Without the app

If you can't access the app any more, email us at **listened.app.support@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my account".

- Send it from the email address you signed up with, or include your username.
- We may ask you to confirm the request before deleting anything.

We process deletion requests within **30 days**.

---

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

- Your account and login details (email address, sign-in method)
- Your profile (username, bio, profile picture, country, favorite genres, top albums and other profile details)
- Your ratings and reviews, and your comments and likes
- Your lists and saved lists
- Your follows and followers
- Your direct messages and conversations
- Your notifications, push notification tokens, feedback and reports you submitted

---

## What we keep, and for how long

- **Backups:** database backups are kept for up to **30 days** and then deleted automatically. Deleted data can remain in these backups until they expire.
- **Mentions by others:** notifications other users received about your activity no longer show your account.

We don't keep any other data after your account is deleted.

---

## Delete only some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove content. You can delete individual reviews, comments and lists, and edit or remove your profile details, directly in the app at any time.
